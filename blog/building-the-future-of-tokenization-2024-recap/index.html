Building the Vision - Stobox’s 2024 Recap 🎄

2024 was a year of growth and innovation for Stobox - new clients, partnerships, and the launch of Stobox 4 Beta. Bigger things ahead!

Stobox Research

31 December 2024 · 7 min read

Looking back, it’s clear this was not only a year of progress. It was a year of bold decisions that reimagined our vision and capabilities, elevating Stobox from a web3-focused tokenization provider to a fully regulated, enterprise ecosystem for asset tokenization solutions.

Let’s take a closer look at how 2024 unfolded…

Over the past five years, Stobox has built a solid foundation in the asset tokenization industry, serving a diverse range of clients across multiple sectors. In 2024 alone, we successfully delivered tokenization solutions to 18 business clients focused on Real-World Assets (RWA). While our existing tokenization product suite - including DS Dashboard, DS Swap, and Soulbound ID - was designed primarily for web3 and small-to-medium enterprises, we recognized the need to evolve.

At the start of 2024, we decided to develop the fourth generation of the Stobox tokenization platform!

This new platform is designed to address the complex needs of enterprise clients while also making tokenization more accessible and adoptable for small and medium-sized businesses.

Learn more about Stobox 4

In May 2024, we expanded the Development Team and began building Stobox 4, designed to:

  • Provide enterprise wallet infrastructure;
  • Enable tokenization of a wide variety of real-world assets;
  • Serve as a unified entry point for tokenization clients and their investors.

Stobox 4 Beta is a blockchain-based platform designed to reshape the management and interaction with digital and tokenized real-world assets (RWAs).

The platform combines advanced blockchain technology with regulatory compliance standards to provide individuals and businesses with secure, streamlined, and versatile solutions.

In November, we reached a major milestone with the launch of Stobox 4 Beta

Stobox 4 Beta is now open to the public, laying the groundwork for user registration and wallet issuance, setting the stage for a powerful tokenization module.

Register at Stobox 4

Key features of Stobox 4 Beta include:

  • Secure Wallet Issuance: Built on Fireblocks Multi-Party Computation (MPC) non-custodial wallet technology, Stobox 4 Beta prioritizes user control while offering a secure wallet environment for various crypto and tokenized assets.
  • Compliant Transactions: The platform ensures that all transactions are KYC/AML verified, allowing secure and transparent transactions only between authenticated users.
  • DeFi Integration: Users will soon be able to swap assets using Web3 decentralized applications (dApps), enhanced through WalletConnect integration, enabling direct access to various DeFi platforms.

The initial asset list available on Stobox 4 Beta includes a limited array of crypto and tokenized RWAs, with plans to expand offerings to include diverse classes of tokenized real-world assets, such as real estate, commodities, and private equity.

This Stobox transformation wasn’t just technological - it required a fundamental shift in our regulatory positioning. Since its formation in 2018, Stobox operated as a technology and service provider. However, the launch of our new products required us to step up and qualify as a Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP).

In December 2024, we secured a VASP license!

This achievement sets the stage for delivering scalable, compliant, and enterprise tokenization services in 2025 and beyond.

Read more

We recognize the rapid evolution of the tokenization industry and are committed to actively shaping its future. This requires not only adapting our products to meet emerging standards but also redefining our business model.

Until now, Stobox has primarily offered turn-key tokenization solutions, focusing heavily on service-based deliveries. While this approach has been effective for small enterprises, it presents limitations in terms of scalability.

To scale, Stobox is transitioning to a product-centric model - one where each product is tailored to specific niches, asset classes, and geographic markets.

Here are some examples:

  • Real Estate Tokenization in Switzerland – Enabling fractional ownership and direct distribution of rental income for residential and commercial properties in Zurich and Geneva.
  • Renewable Energy Project Financing in Great Britain and Scandinavia – Facilitating investments in solar farms, wind parks, and green energy initiatives to drive sustainable development.
  • Private Equity Funds in the United States – Unlocking access to diversified portfolios for investors in cities like New York, Miami, and San Francisco, while providing enhanced liquidity and lower entry barriers.

These use-case-driven solutions will be a key component of Stobox 4’s tokenization module, scheduled for release in Q1 2025. Designed to provide issuers with clear, step-by-step frameworks, the module will simplify the process of asset tokenization on the platform. This transition to scalable, product-focused solutions not only accelerates our growth potential but also reinforces investor confidence as we continue to expand and innovate.

We believe Stobox 4 is set to become a benchmark for Real-World Asset (RWA) tokenization - excelling in product innovation, regulatory compliance, and market adoption.

Register at Stobox 4

With thousands of businesses worldwide exploring tokenization, the high costs of current technologies and service-based models often create barriers to entry. At Stobox, we are committed to breaking down these barriers by delivering a direct and cost-effective tokenization framework.

To achieve these ambitious goals, our team is developing several key technologies to provide a complete, end-to-end onboarding experience for tokenization:

  • Stobox 4 Tokenization Platform – A unified ecosystem designed for scalability and enterprise solutions.
  • Stobox DID (Decentralized Identity) – Ensuring secure, verifiable identities for compliant transactions.
  • STV3 Protocol – Enabling the issuance and management of compliant tokenized digital assets.

We remain focused on execution, actively refining our roadmap and providing regular updates on new features and releases as we continue to shape the future of tokenization.

Stobox Joins Blockchain for Europe Association in 2024

 Stobox became a proud member of the Blockchain for Europe Association, a leading organization dedicated to advancing blockchain adoption and innovation across the EU. 

Read more

Joining this influential network provides Stobox with valuable opportunities to promote our vision and products, collaborate with industry leaders, and support the broader adoption of tokenization technologies.

Our involvement focuses on advancing discussions around asset tokenization while strengthening Stobox’s position among top players in the industry. Looking ahead to 2025, we plan to actively contribute to the association’s initiatives and share our latest developments with its members.

2024 has been a landmark year for Stobox, marked by the new product launch, strategic advancements, and a strong focus on asset tokenization...

We have been highly proactive in strengthening our ecosystem, expanding the utility of our native token, Stobox Token (STBU), and delivering exceptional performance in tokenization services. Throughout the year, we signed 18 new clients, formed strategic partnerships, and participated in key industry events to enhance visibility and market influence.

Here are some of the Key Milestones in 2024:

Business Expansion and Partnerships

  • Signed 18 B2B Clients – Expanded the client base with businesses seeking RWA tokenization solutions.

  • Formed 10+ Strategic Partnerships with industry leaders, including:

    • Fireblocks – Enterprise wallet infrastructure with MPC technology.
    • Stasis – Collaboration to advance stablecoin integration.
    • Defactor – Strengthening DeFi capabilities.
    • Chainlink – Integration with CCIP for secure, cross-chain interoperability.
    • Fractal ID – Enhancing identity verification and compliance standards.

Product and Technology Development

  • Launched Stobox 4 Beta - Released the new tokenization platform after 8 months of development, providing the foundation for scalable and compliant tokenization services.
  • Integrated Chainlink CCIP - Enabled secure cross-chain transactions for tokenized assets.
  • Introduced STBU Staking Programs - Provided reward opportunities for token holders.
  • Launched STBU Crypto Mastercard - Introduced payment cards through FNT Bank, bridging traditional and crypto finance.

Regulatory Milestones

  • Secured VASP License – Achieved a Virtual Asset Service Provider license in December 2024, enabling regulated tokenization services.
  • Nominated as Best Regulatory Innovations – Recognized at Paris Blockchain Week for regulatory leadership.

Marketing and Industry Engagement

  • Joined Blockchain for Europe Association – Became a member of a leading EU blockchain network to influence policy and adoption.
  • Featured in CoinGecko RWA Global Research – Recognized as a key player in RWA tokenization.
  • Attended 8+ Offline Conferences – Represented Stobox at global blockchain events.
  • Team spoke at Web Summit – Stobox team participated as a speaker and was featured among the best Series-A startups.
  • Reached 60,000+ YouTube Followers – Expanded the Stobox audience through educational and promotional content.

Ecosystem Growth

  • Listed STBU on MEXC Exchange – Enhanced token accessibility and liquidity.
  • Expanded Utilities for STBU – Focused on embedding STBU into the Stobox 4 ecosystem for payments, subscriptions, and platform engagements.
  • Expanded STBU Liquidity – Increased the Uniswap V3 STBU pool liquidity to $500k.

Wishing you a prosperous, innovative, and successful year ahead! Happy New Year from all of us at Stobox!
