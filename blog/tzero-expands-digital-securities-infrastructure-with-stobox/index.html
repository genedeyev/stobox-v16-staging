tZERO Expands Digital Securities Infrastructure with Stobox

The tokenization industry is moving quickly. More companies are exploring digital securities and real-world asset tokenization as a new way to access capital markets.

Stobox Research

31 March 2026 · 5 min read

The tokenization industry is moving quickly. More companies are exploring digital securities and real-world asset tokenization as a new way to access capital markets.

However, one major challenge still remains.Issuing a tokenized security is only the first step. What matters next is distribution and the ability for those securities to operate within regulated market infrastructure.

Without a clear pathway from primary issuance to secondary market activity, many tokenization projects struggle to reach their full potential. This is the challenge Stobox and tZERO aim to address.

Stobox and tZERO have signed a Memorandum of Understanding to explore a stronger alignment between tokenization infrastructure and regulated brokerage technology.

The goal is simple. Create a more connected environment where digital securities can move from issuance to market infrastructure in a structured and compliant way. This approach addresses one of the most important challenges in the RWA industry today: moving from compliant token issuance to real market participation, liquidity, and long-term investor engagement.

Connecting Issuance and Market Infrastructure

Stobox focuses on the infrastructure needed to bring real-world assets onchain.

  • The Stobox Tokenization Framework and onboarding methodology, designed to structure real businesses and assets for regulated tokenization
  • The STV3 programmable assets protocol, enabling compliant and flexible asset logic
  • Stobox DID, supporting verified identities and issuer validation
  • The Stobox 4 platform, delivering an all-in-one environment for issuing, managing, and scaling tokenized RWAs

Together, these components allow Stobox to work with verified, compliant issuers and prepare them for interaction with regulated market infrastructure such as tZERO.

tZERO operates a regulated brokerage and trading infrastructure in the United States, supporting digital securities activity through its broker-dealer and alternative trading system.

By aligning these two layers of infrastructure, issuers may be able to benefit from both environments as the digital securities market evolves.

Expanding Opportunities for Issuers

For companies exploring tokenization, access to infrastructure across both stages of the asset lifecycle is critical.

Stobox provides the environment for structuring and launching digital securities offerings. tZERO brings experience in regulated brokerage operations and trading infrastructure.

Together, the two organizations are exploring ways to support issuers that want both a robust primary issuance environment and visibility within regulated market infrastructure. Each organization continues to operate independently and provides services directly to issuers within its respective scope.

Building a Vertical Tokenization Infrastructure

At Stobox, our long-term vision is to build a vertical infrastructure for digital securities.

This means supporting the entire lifecycle of tokenized assets from the earliest stages of preparation to market infrastructure. The process begins with business structuring and tokenization onboarding, where companies prepare their assets, legal structures, and compliance frameworks. It continues with primary issuance and investor onboarding, supported by the Stobox platform and programmable asset infrastructure.

The next stage is distribution and market access, where digital securities become visible within broader financial ecosystems. Finally, the lifecycle extends to regulated secondary market environments, where assets can operate within structured trading infrastructure.

The alignment with tZERO reflects this vertical approach. By connecting tokenization infrastructure with regulated brokerage technology, both organizations are working toward a more complete ecosystem for digital securities.

A Step Forward for the Digital Securities Ecosystem

The tokenization market is often described as a multi-trillion-dollar opportunity. But realizing that potential requires more than technology.

It requires coordination across platforms, compliance frameworks, and regulated market infrastructure. The alignment between Stobox and tZERO represents a step toward that coordinated ecosystem.

By connecting tokenization infrastructure with brokerage and trading environments, both organizations aim to bring stronger infrastructure to the evolving digital securities industry.

As the tokenization and RWA sector matures, the industry is moving beyond isolated solutions toward integrated, end-to-end models that combine compliant issuance, investor onboarding, and access to primary and secondary markets. The Stobox and tZERO collaboration is designed to explore exactly this more complex and value-driven approach.

About Stobox

Stobox is a licensed and regulated tokenization provider building infrastructure for real-world assets. The company offers a complete ecosystem for issuing and managing tokenized assets, including the STV3 Programmable Assets Protocol, Stobox 4 platform, and Stobox DID identity infrastructure. To date, Stobox has tokenized more than 300 million USD in assets for over 100 clients across industries including finance, energy, mining, and real estate.

About tZERO

tZERO Group, Inc. (tZERO) and its broker-dealer subsidiaries provide an innovative liquidity platform for private companies and assets. We offer institutional-grade solutions for issuers looking to digitize their capital table through blockchain technology, and make such equity available for trading on an alternative trading system. tZERO, through its broker-dealer subsidiaries, democratizes access to private assets by providing a simple, automated, and efficient trading venue to broker-dealers, institutions, and investors. All technology services are offered through tZERO Technologies, LLC. For more information, please visit our website. 

About tZERO Digital Asset Securities, LLC

tZERO Digital Asset Securities, LLC is a broker-dealer registered with the SEC and a member of FINRA and SIPC. It is the broker-dealer custodian of all digital asset securities offered on tZERO’s online brokerage platform.  More information about tZERO Digital Asset Securities may be found on FINRA’s BrokerCheck.    

About tZERO Securities, LLC

tZERO Securities, LLC is a broker-dealer registered with the SEC and a member of FINRA and SIPC. It is the operator of the tZERO Securities ATS. More information about tZERO Securities may be found on FINRA’s BrokerCheck.
