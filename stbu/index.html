STBU · the Stobox ecosystem token

The key to everything Stobox, held in your own wallet.

STBU is the utility token of the Stobox ecosystem. Holding it lowers your fees across Stobox products, earns badges that make every swap cheaper, and opens the seasons and programmes that run on the STBU portal. It is not equity and it is not a security. One contract on Base, from 15 September 2026.

Join the Telegram room Where to get it → Follow on X →

Utility token · Base · swap opens 15 September 2026 · 115,450,899 outstanding on the legacy contracts today

Not open yet.

The portal swap and the Uniswap pool both open on 15 September 2026, together with the claim. Until then STBU can only be burned on its legacy chains; it cannot be bought anywhere. Anyone offering it before that date is not us.

What it does for you

Six things a wallet holding STBU gets.

Every one of them is read off the wallet itself. There is nothing to register, nothing to hand over and nothing to lock.

  • 01

    Lower fees on Stobox products

    Hold STBU and pay less for tokenization and the other Stobox products. The discount is read off your wallet, not off a coupon.

  • 02

    A cheaper swap, the more you take part

    The pool fee runs from 1% down to 0.1%. Where you sit in that range depends on the badges your wallet has earned, and the portal applies it automatically.

  • 03

    Badges you earn, and badges you can trade

    Earned badges are soulbound: they stay with the wallet that did the work. A second line of badges is tradable. Both raise your tier.

  • 04

    Seasons and programmes

    Points, seasons and community programmes run on the portal. Season one opens with the claim on 15 September. What each season rewards is announced on X and in Telegram before it starts.

  • 05

    The Investor badge

    A wallet holding US$1,000 or more of STBU earns the Investor badge. A verified identity adds points; it never gates a tier.

  • 06

    One account for everything Stobox

    The login on the STBU portal is the same as on app.stobox.io. A holder is a Stobox user, with Intelligence, Raisable and Compass one click away.

The migration

Four steps, one of them yours.

Legacy STBU moves to Base by burn and claim, one for one, with no bridge. You burn once on the chain you already hold on; the claim opens by itself.

Live today

Four legacy contracts

STBU sits on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon and Arbitrum. Each contract holds part of the supply and part of the liquidity, which is why a trade of any size moves the price more than it should.

Before 15 Sep 2026

Burn on your origin chain

One transaction on whichever chain holds your tokens: send them to the burn address. The indexer records it. Nothing is sent to Stobox and nobody takes custody.

From 15 Sep 2026

Claim on Base

The same wallet claims the same amount on the Base contract, at stbu.stobox.io. The claim opens by itself at 00:00 UTC; the burn window closes at the same moment.

From 15 Sep 2026

Swap, badges, season one

The STBU pool opens on Uniswap, the portal's swap goes live with the dynamic fee, and season one of the badges starts counting.

Straight answers

The questions that decide it.

What is STBU for?

It is the key to the Stobox ecosystem: lower fees on Stobox products, a cheaper swap the more you take part, badges and seasons on the STBU portal, and one login shared with app.stobox.io. It is a utility token. It carries no ownership of Stobox.

Where can I get STBU?

Only from one public pool on Uniswap, on Base, against USDT: through the swap on stbu.stobox.io or on Uniswap directly. Neither is open yet. Trading opens on 15 September 2026 with the claim.

Is STBU live on Base yet?

The contract is deployed and reads a supply of zero, which is correct: it mints only against verified burns, and claiming opens on 15 September 2026 at 00:00 UTC.

I hold legacy STBU. What do I do?

Send it to the burn address on the chain you hold it on, before 15 September. From 15 September the same wallet claims the same amount on Base. If you do nothing, your tokens stay on the legacy contract.

Does Stobox hold my tokens at any point?

No. You burn from your own wallet and claim to your own wallet. The portal is a self-custodial software interface; the pool is a public pool on Uniswap.

Is the supply changing?

Every burned token is claimable one for one. The contract on Base is capped at 250,000,000 and mints only against a verified burn; the table above reads the legacy balances off the contracts themselves.

Is STBU equity in Stobox?

No. STBU is a utility token. STBX is the equity: a regulated security on Arbitrum, a separate instrument under separate rules.

Two ways in

The equity is a different instrument.

STBU is a utility token and carries no ownership. STBX is regulated Class-C equity, live on-chain since 2025, under its own documents.
