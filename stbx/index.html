Why it exists

The only demonstration that costs us something.

STBX runs on Stobox Compass, the same compliant tokenization stack we provide to issuers. Anyone can describe their infrastructure. We put our own cap table on ours, which means a defect in it is a defect in our own ownership record.

  • The same stack as clients

    Not a demonstration environment and not a fork. The registry, the eligibility checks and the transfer rules are the ones an issuer gets.

  • An on-chain registry

    Who holds what is recorded where the token lives, rather than reconciled afterwards against a spreadsheet.

  • Compliance inside the asset

    The token refuses a transfer to a wallet that is not eligible. The rule does not depend on anyone remembering to apply it.

Straight answers

The questions that decide it.

Is STBX a security?

Yes, and that is the point. It represents Class-C shares in Stobox Technologies, Inc., issued by Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd, and it carries the rights, restrictions and disclosures of a regulated security. Treat it the way you would treat any private share purchase.

How is this different from the Reg D round?

Same underlying ownership, different rails and different investors. The Reg D 506(c) offering runs through a FINRA-registered broker-dealer for verified US accredited investors, under its own documents. STBX is a private placement of our own equity to qualified non-US investors, offered directly by the issuer.

Why tokenize your own equity?

Because we sell that stack to other people. STBX runs on Stobox Compass, the same compliant tokenization stack we provide to issuers. Putting our own cap table on it is the only demonstration that costs us something.

Can I sell it?

Only to an eligible wallet, and only where regulation permits. There is a path to secondary trading through licensed venues, and the venue decides whether to list. Assume illiquidity.

What do I actually sign?

The Offering Documents on Stobox Compass. Everything on this page is a description of them, and where the two differ the documents govern.
