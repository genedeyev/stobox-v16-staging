What a company valuation actually is

A valuation is a defensible estimate of what a business is worth today. For a private company like Stobox, there is no public stock price – so an independent, methodical estimate is how you put a number on it.

Market approach

What comparable companies are worth

Benchmarks a business against real funding rounds, secondary transactions, and revenue multiples of comparable private companies. This is the primary basis Eqvista uses for Stobox.

Income approach

What future cash flows are worth

Discounts expected future earnings back to today. Weighted more heavily for mature, cash-generating businesses; lighter for early-growth technology companies.

Asset approach

What the net assets are worth

Values a company as the sum of its assets less liabilities. A floor reference for asset-heavy businesses; less central for a software and infrastructure company.

A formal appraisal – such as a 409A or a fund-raising valuation – is usually a static PDF that is months old by the time anyone reads it, and a 409A in particular is prepared for tax and financial-reporting purposes only. A real-time valuation is a different, continuously-updated market-based estimate that tracks the company's data and the market, the way a public share price does – a professional estimate, not a live quote and not a 409A.

What this valuation is – and is not

It is

  • An independent, third-party market-based valuation estimate of the company (Eqvista Real-Time Company Valuation®).
  • Produced by Eqvista, and made shareable by Eqvista as a Real-Time Company Valuation®.
  • Updated as company and market inputs change, with each mark's effective date shown.
  • Informational context for understanding Stobox as a business.

It is not

  • An objective fact – a valuation is a subjective professional opinion of value; reasonable analysts applying the same standards could reach a different number.
  • The company's 409A fair market value – a separate appraisal for tax and financial reporting (detailed on our blog), determined independently and lower than this estimate.
  • The price of the STBX security token, or any offering price.
  • A tradeable, quoted, or guaranteed market price.
  • An offer, a solicitation, investment advice, or a promise of future value or returns.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Stobox Technologies Inc.'s valuation?+

As of Jul 14, 2026, 03:11 PM ET, the Eqvista Real-Time Company Valuation® of Stobox Technologies Inc. is $17,383,677, with an implied value of $1.74 per share (the estimate divided by shares outstanding). It is a market-based estimate – not the company's 409A fair market value, not an offering price, and not a tradeable market price.

Who produced the Stobox valuation and how?+

The figure is produced by Eqvista, an independent equity-management and valuation platform. Its Real-Time Company Valuation® combines AI/ML models trained on $300B+ in assets and calibration against live public and private-market comparables, and updates continuously as inputs change, rather than as a static annual report.

Is this the same as Stobox's 409A valuation?+

No. This is Eqvista's Real-Time Company Valuation® – a continuously-updated, market-based estimate that Eqvista makes shareable. It is separate from the company's 409A valuation, an independent appraisal prepared solely for IRC Section 409A and financial-reporting/tax purposes. The 409A is a confidential document whose contents are not published; its fair market value of the common stock, used for option pricing and tax, is determined separately from this estimate. Both are subjective professional opinions of value, not statements of objective fact.

Is the Stobox valuation the same as the STBX token price?+

No. The figure is Eqvista's Real-Time Company Valuation® of the company – a market-based estimate. STBX is a separate regulated security token representing Class-C equity issued by Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd, offered only through Offering Documents to eligible investors. The estimate is not the STBX offering price and not a quoted or tradeable price for any token or share.

Can I buy shares in Stobox at this valuation?+

No. This page is informational only. It is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security, and no security may be purchased on the basis of this valuation. Any offering is made solely through official Offering Documents and licensed channels, to eligible investors, where permitted.

How often is the valuation updated?+

Eqvista updates the valuation continuously as company and market inputs change. The figure published here is refreshed from Eqvista's confirmed marks; the effective date is shown alongside the number.
