Presenting Stobox 4 Beta on Websummit 2024

Stobox, a leader in tokenization, is set to showcase its Stobox 4 Beta at Web Summit, highlighting its innovation in tokenized asset solutions.

Stobox Research

25 October 2024 · 2 min read

Stobox, a recognized leader in the tokenization industry, is gearing up to make waves at Websummit 2024, one of the world's largest and most influential tech events, with a stellar reputation built over six years, servicing more than 70 clients and tokenizing over $305Million in assets for its clients.

This year, we will launch the beta of its much-anticipated Stobox 4 from the Websummit Stage, aiming to capture the attention of market professionals from across the globe.

Stobox 4: Pioneering the Future of Tokenization

At the core of Stobox's participation is the exposure of Stobox 4, an innovative technology platform that promises to redefine how businesses approach tokenization. This product, which will enter its beta phase during Websummit, provides one window for RWAs, addressing key challenges businesses face when entering the digital asset space. Stobox 4 is built utilizing Fireblocks MPC NCW (non-custodial-wallets) and integrations with Fractal ID, Circle, EURS, Artbitrium, and many others and first partners. 

The launch is particularly significant because it demonstrates Stobox's commitment to providing a solution for businesses never built by anyone, looking to use tokenized securities. By launching Stobox 4 at Websummit, Stobox aims to foster deeper connections with industry leaders, investors, and innovators, ensuring its continued role as a dominant force in the world of digital assets.

Stobox - Among the Best of Series-A Startups

Websummit’s Startup Programme has recognized Stobox as one of the top Series-A startups to join the event. This selection places the company in a prime location at the event, with a booth next to the main stage, providing the best networking opportunities. The Stobox team will be on hand to showcase a demo of the Stobox 4 technology, exchange insights with innovators, and engage with potential clients and partners.

Founders to Speak at the Growth Stage

Ross Shemeliak and Gene Deyev, Founders of Stobox, will give a speech at the prestigious Growth Stage. This session is expected to focus on digital asset evolution, the rise of tokenized securities, and how Stobox is reshaping this space through technological innovations. Based on the company's success and real-world use cases, Ross's insights will provide invaluable knowledge to entrepreneurs, investors, and tech enthusiasts attending Websummit.

Websummit: A Launchpad for Industry Giants

Websummit has historically been a launchpad for some of the world’s most influential tech companies. Many notable firms have unveiled groundbreaking products at past editions of the conference, gaining significant traction from the global tech community. For example, in previous years, Uber showcased its international expansion plans at Websummit, Tesla demonstrated advanced advancements in autonomous driving, and Stripe introduced major product updates that solidified its role as a financial technology leader.

By launching Stobox 4 during this highly anticipated event, Stobox joins the ranks of these tech giants, aiming to harness the momentum of Websummit’s global reach to drive future growth.

Aiming for Global Impact

With the eyes of the world on Websummit 2024, Stobox is strategically positioned to drive significant traction for Stobox 4, attracting market professionals, venture capitalists, and industry thought leaders.

The beta launch is not only a product release; it is a statement of Stobox’s ambition to lead the tokenization space and empower businesses to integrate directly into the world of tokenized assets.
