What we do not do

Said once, here, so no page has to say it twice.

These are the shape of the business, not caveats added at the end.

  • ×

    Not a broker-dealer

    Any regulated sale runs through a licensed firm, and any commission belongs to them.

  • ×

    Not a law firm

    Which exemption applies is decided by qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.

  • ×

    Never a percentage

    No success fee and no carry, at any size, at any layer. We never take a cut of what you raise.

  • ×

    No liquidity promise

    You get a register, eligibility rules and a settlement unit. Listing decisions rest solely with the venue.

  • ×

    No timeline in weeks

    Issuance is set by counsel, by a lender’s consent and by a valuer’s calendar, none of which we control.

  • ×

    No hidden costs

    Counsel, audit, custody and filing fees are paid direct to those firms. We do not mark them up.
