The Economy of Trust: How Ukraine's Municipalities, Global Partners, and Tokenization Are Rewiring Post-War Reconstruction

Economy of Trust Ukraine unites mayors, a coalition of international companies, and a 7-stage project finance program to turn Ukraine's reconstruction into transparent, investable assets. Stobox serves as the ecosystem's tokenization partner. Here is how the model works, who is behind it, and why verifiable transparency, not promises, is what will unlock capital for Ukraine. Available in English and Ukrainian.

By Gene Deyev, Founder and Chief Executive of Stobox

23 July 2026 · 39 min read

Rebuilding a country is not one project. It is thousands of projects across hundreds of municipalities, each needing capital, engineering, governance, and above all, trust. That is the problem Economy of Trust Ukraine exists to solve, and it is why Stobox serves as the ecosystem's tokenization partner, bringing eight years of regulated digital-securities infrastructure to one of the most consequential reconstruction efforts of our time. This article explains what Economy of Trust Ukraine is, how its municipal-led model works, who stands behind it, and how tokenization turns transparency from a promise into a verifiable property of every dollar invested in Ukraine. × TOKENIZATION PARTNER ## Why transparency decides whether Ukraine gets rebuilt Ukraine's reconstruction is the largest infrastructure undertaking Europe has seen since the Marshall Plan. The World Bank's joint damage assessment puts the cost of recovery and reconstruction at more than half a trillion dollars over the coming decade, several times Ukraine's annual GDP. No single government, donor institution, or development bank can carry that number alone. The gap will be closed by private capital, or it will not be closed at all. And private capital asks one question before any other. Not "what is the return?" Returns in post-war rebuilding economies have historically been substantial. The first question is always: how will I know where my money went? Every institutional allocator, family office, and diaspora investor weighing an investment in Ukraine's recovery carries the same two concerns: corruption risk and verification cost. Reports can be written; audits arrive months late; intermediaries multiply. In a conventional structure, transparency is something you are told about. In a tokenized structure, transparency is something you can check yourself, at any time, on infrastructure nobody can quietly edit. That is the thesis on which Economy of Trust Ukraine and Stobox align, and it is worth stating in the founder's own words. > "Every conversation about investing in Ukraine starts with the same question. Not 'what is the return?' but 'how will I know where my money went?' That is the right question, and for decades the honest answer everywhere in emerging markets was: you won't, not fully. Tokenization changes the physics of that answer. When a municipal project is financed through tokenized instruments, transparency stops being a quarterly PDF and becomes a property of the capital itself: every commitment, every milestone, every disbursement is written to a ledger that nobody can quietly edit. That is what Stobox delivers to the Economy of Trust ecosystem: infrastructure where trust is not requested from investors. It is proven to them, block by block. Ukraine's reconstruction will be the largest test of programmable trust in history, and the country that proves it works will export that standard to the world." > > – Gene Deyev, Founder &amp; CEO, Stobox ## What is Economy of Trust Ukraine? Economy of Trust Ukraine (EoTU) is a platform that connects Ukraine with the world "through transparency, governance, and efficiency for all stakeholders", with a stated ambition of uniting 100,000,000 people in support of Ukraine, under the motto All for Ukraine – for All of Ukraine. It was founded and is led by CEO Henry Shterenberg, who is also Honorary President of World Trade Center Kyiv. EoTU's mission is to accelerate Ukraine's shift from a legacy, centrally planned economic structure to a decentralized, 21st-century market economy, and to do it from the bottom up through municipal-led decentralization. Its initial focus is SMART infrastructure for the intelligent Ukrainian cities of the future. The ecosystem is built from several interlocking parts: – The Mayors' Club Ukraine and its Investment Committee: a municipal authority, chaired by Henry Shterenberg, that vets projects for genuine municipal commitment, economic viability, and alignment with Ukraine's decentralization reforms before they enter the platform. – A municipal network spanning cities such as Mykolaiv, Rivne, Ternopil, Uzhhorod, Khmelnytsky, Vinnytsia, Melitopol, Lutsk, Poltava, and Chernihiv, each represented on the platform by its mayor. – The SMART Infrastructure International Coalition (SIIC): several hundred international and Ukrainian companies aligned to standardize and execute the full smart-city project lifecycle, from concept through operation. – A Global Network of Business Development Ambassadors: pre-qualified international relationships that lower entry barriers for foreign investors and companies entering Ukraine's rebuilding market. – The Economy of Trust Academy and UMEDA (the Ukrainian Municipal Economic Development Agency, led by Yuliya Chufistova), plus bi-weekly expert info sessions, international trade missions under the REBIRTH OF UKRAINE banner, and a weekly newsletter reaching more than 50,000 subscribers. Every member of the ecosystem commits to a common Code of Ethics, a detail that sounds ceremonial until you remember what the platform is named after. Trust is the product. Everything else is delivery. ## Why municipalities lead, and why that matters for investors Most reconstruction frameworks are designed top-down: national programs, ministry pipelines, donor conferences. EoTU inverts the model, and the inversion is not ideological. It is practical. Ukraine's decentralization reform, underway since 2014, moved real budgets and real decision-making power to territorial communities (hromadas). Mayors know which bridge is out, which industrial site has rail access, which school needs a shelter, and which investor promises were kept. A reconstruction model that starts with municipalities starts with the layer of government closest to both the need and the accountability. For investors, this changes the risk profile in a specific way: every project on the platform arrives with an owner. The Mayors' Club Investment Committee (uniting prominent mayors, investment advisors, business representatives, and industry specialists) functions as a municipal underwriting body. A project does not enter the platform because someone drew a rendering; it enters because a community government has committed to it, and a committee of its peers has judged it economically viable. ## A real deal flow, not a concept deck What makes EoTU notable is that its pipeline is concrete and staged. The published deal flow already spans more than two dozen named projects: – "2040: Our Future Horizon" master plans: long-range development visions for communities including Stryi, Drohobych, Balta, Podilsk, Slavuta, Balakliya, Khodoriv, Ratne, Medenychi, Novyi Buh, and a dozen more. – The Carpathian Regional Economic Development Zone (CREZ): 26 municipalities anchored by the city of Stryi, currently at feasibility stage. – A Network of Industrial Parks, including the 23-hectare Eco-Mukachevo industrial park in Zakarpattia, already at construction stage. – Flagship urban projects: World Trade Center Kyiv (the ecosystem's future worldwide headquarters), the Golden Gate of Kyiv hotel, New Era Sports &amp; Academics, and Academic City, a plan to establish Ukraine as a global education and innovation hub. – Sector programs: hydrogen generation (aligned with Ukraine's draft National Hydrogen Strategy targeting 10–15 GW of electrolyzer capacity), sustainable water (the Stryi Sustainable Water Action Plan), and circular economy. Each project is tagged with its lifecycle stage (concept, pre-feasibility, feasibility, development, construction) and moves forward under the Investment Committee's oversight. This staging discipline matters enormously for what comes next, because it is exactly the structure that milestone-gated finance needs. ## The 7-Stage Project Finance Program: where tokenization comes in The hardest part of reconstruction finance is not finding worthy projects; Ukraine has thousands. It is structuring them so that international capital can participate with confidence, at scale, and without a due-diligence cost that eats the return. EoTU's answer is its 7-Stage Project Finance Program: a structured lifecycle that transforms Nationwide Strategic Initiatives into tokenized, investable assets with blockchain-level transparency and milestone-gated liquidity. Three properties of this model deserve emphasis, because together they answer the transparency question head-on: 1. Transparency by design. Every funding milestone, disbursement, and ownership position lives on-chain. For a country rebuilding under intense international scrutiny, this is not a nice-to-have. It is the core product. An investor in Toronto, a donor institution in Brussels, and a mayor in Stryi are all looking at the same record, and none of them can alter it. It is, quite literally, an economy of trust. 2. Milestone-gated capital. Funds unlock as projects hit verified stages of the lifecycle, aligning investor protection with construction reality and replacing lump-sum risk with staged, auditable commitments. If a stage is not verified, the next tranche does not move. This is the single most effective anti-corruption mechanism ever built into project finance, because it does not rely on anyone's honesty, only on evidence. 3. Global access under compliance. Tokenized instruments let a member of the eight-million-strong Ukrainian diaspora, a European family office, and an institutional investor participate in the same project under the same transparent rules, each onboarded through the KYC/AML and eligibility framework their jurisdiction requires. Compliance is not a gate bolted on afterward; it is enforced at the token level. ## How Stobox delivers it: the transparency stack Stobox has been building regulated tokenization infrastructure since 2018: more than $305M in assets structured and supported, clients across 20+ jurisdictions, and active backing of ERC-7943 (uRWA), the emerging interoperability standard for compliant real-world-asset tokens. (This is an illustration of the financing mechanics, not a description of any live offering.) Stages one and two: mandate and vetting. The Stryi city council commits to the module: land allocation, tariff framework, operational responsibility. The Mayors' Club Investment Committee reviews the commitment, the demand data, and the municipality's track record, and admits the project to the platform. At this point the project already has what most emerging-market infrastructure deals never get: a politically accountable owner and a peer-reviewed mandate. Stages three and four: engineering and structure. Coalition engineers (this is where a partner like Sweco or PureBlue enters) produce the feasibility study: capacity, capex, construction phases, operating economics. Legal counsel structures the project vehicle, and Stobox issues the tokenized instrument on top of it: a compliant digital security whose transfer rules, investor eligibility, and disclosure obligations are encoded at the token level. Stage five: the raise. Investors (a Ukrainian-American engineer in Chicago, a family office in Vienna, an impact fund in London) onboard through KYC/AML, each screened against the rules of their own jurisdiction. Every subscription is recorded on-chain. Nobody's participation depends on knowing the right people in the right office. Stage six: construction, tranche by tranche. Capital does not transfer as a lump sum. When the permitting milestone is verified, a tranche releases. When equipment is delivered and inspected, another tranche releases. Each release is a transaction on a public ledger, tied to the evidence that unlocked it. If a milestone slips, capital waits, visibly. The incentive structure this creates on the ground (for contractors, for officials, for everyone) is the quiet revolution inside the loud one. Stage seven: operation. The plant runs. Revenue, maintenance events, and distributions are reported on the same rails the capital came in on. Five years later, a new investor doing due diligence does not request a data room: the material history of the asset is already public, continuous, and tamper-evident. Now multiply that by the 26 municipalities of the Carpathian zone, by the industrial parks network, by every "2040" master plan in the pipeline. That is what "tokenized, investable assets with blockchain-level transparency and milestone-gated liquidity" means when it stops being a phrase and starts being plumbing. ## The coalition behind the platform A platform like this only works if the full project lifecycle is covered: engineering, water, energy, connectivity, law, risk, and capital. Місія EoTU – прискорити перехід України від спадщини централізовано планованої економіки до децентралізованої ринкової економіки XXI століття, і зробити це знизу вгору, через децентралізацію під проводом муніципалітетів. Початковий фокус – SMART-інфраструктура для майбутніх інтелектуальних міст України. What is Economy of Trust Ukraine?

Economy of Trust Ukraine (EoTU) is a platform founded by Henry Shterenberg that connects Ukrainian municipalities with international companies and investors to finance post-war reconstruction. It combines a mayors' network with an investment committee, a coalition of several hundred international and Ukrainian companies, and a structured project finance program, with a focus on smart infrastructure for Ukraine's cities.

What does it mean that Stobox is the tokenization partner of Economy of Trust Ukraine?

Stobox provides the digital-securities infrastructure for the ecosystem's project finance model: compliant token issuance, investor onboarding with KYC/AML, milestone-gated capital logic, and on-chain lifecycle reporting. Stobox has built regulated tokenization infrastructure since 2018, with more than $305M in assets structured and supported across 20+ countries, and backs the ERC-7943 (uRWA) standard for compliant real-world-asset tokens.

How does tokenization make investing in Ukraine's reconstruction transparent?

When a project is financed through tokenized instruments, ownership, funding milestones, and disbursements are recorded on a public blockchain. Investors can verify where capital went and what it built without relying on after-the-fact reports, and funds can be released stage by stage only as verified milestones are reached. Transparency becomes a property of the infrastructure rather than a promise.

What is milestone-gated financing?

Milestone-gated financing releases capital in tranches tied to verified project stages (for example feasibility, permitting, construction phases, and commissioning) instead of transferring the full amount upfront. Combined with tokenization, each release is recorded on-chain, which aligns investor protection with construction reality and creates an immutable audit trail.

What kinds of projects are in the Economy of Trust Ukraine pipeline?

The published deal flow spans long-range municipal master plans (the '2040: Our Future Horizon' series for communities such as Stryi, Drohobych, and Balta), the 26-municipality Carpathian Regional Economic Development Zone, a network of industrial parks including Eco-Mukachevo, flagship urban projects such as World Trade Center Kyiv, and sector programs in hydrogen, water, and circular economy.

Why do municipalities lead the Economy of Trust model?

Ukraine's decentralization reforms gave territorial communities real budgets and decision-making power, and mayors know exactly what their cities need. EoTU builds on this: its Mayors' Club Investment Committee vets projects for genuine municipal commitment and economic viability before they enter the platform, so capital meets projects with local ownership and accountability.

Who can participate in financing Ukraine reconstruction projects?

Participation depends on each project's legal structure and the investor's jurisdiction. Tokenized instruments allow international investors, diaspora, family offices, and institutions to be onboarded under the compliance framework applicable to them. Nothing in this article is an offer of securities; each project defines its own terms and eligibility.

How can a company join the Economy of Trust Ukraine coalition?

EoTU runs the SMART Infrastructure International Coalition and a global network of Business Development Ambassadors, and accepts partners across engineering, technology, energy, water, law, finance, and diplomacy. Companies can reach the team through economyoftrustua.com; tokenization-related inquiries can also come through Stobox.
