Fireblocks-Powered MPC Technology

Security forms the foundation of any business financial system, and the Stobox Business Wallet uses the same infrastructure trusted by BlackRock, Revolut, and other major financial institutions. Built on Fireblocks' Multi-Party Computation (MPC) technology, the wallet provides institutional-grade security without sacrificing usability.

Traditional cryptocurrency wallets store private keys in single locations, creating catastrophic failure points. If someone gains access to your private key, they control your funds completely. MPC technology eliminates this vulnerability by splitting key generation and transaction signing across multiple secure parties.

Here's how it works in practice: when you create a Stobox Business Wallet, no single entity ever possesses your complete private key. Instead, the key generation process occurs collaboratively between your device and Fireblocks' secure infrastructure. Neither party can access your funds independently - both must participate in every transaction.

This architecture provides multiple critical advantages for businesses. First, it eliminates the catastrophic risk of total fund loss from compromised devices or stolen credentials. Second, it enables sophisticated access controls and approval workflows that enterprises require. Third, it provides disaster recovery capabilities that single-key systems cannot match.

The MPC infrastructure also supports advanced features like transaction policy enforcement. You can configure rules that require multiple approvals for large transactions, restrict certain types of operations, or implement time-based controls. These policies execute at the cryptographic level, making them impossible to bypass through software manipulation.

Fireblocks' infrastructure maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications, providing the audit trail and security documentation that enterprise compliance teams require. The system includes comprehensive monitoring, alerting, and incident response capabilities that integrate with your existing security operations.
