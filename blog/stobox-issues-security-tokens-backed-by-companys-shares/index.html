Stobox Reaches a Major Milestone: Tokenized Assets Now Live on Stobox 4

Stobox 4.2.1 introduces the complete tokenization cycle - from asset setup to investor verification with live tokenized assets and STBX tokens now live on Arbitrum.

Stobox Research

15 October 2025 · 4 min read

We’re excited to announce the next big step in Stobox’s journey - tokenized assets are now live on the Stobox 4 Platform.

With the release of Stobox 4.2.1, we’ve reached a defining moment in our mission to make compliant, real-world tokenization accessible to businesses and investors worldwide.

The Complete Tokenization Cycle Is Here

The latest Stobox 4.2.1 Update marks the completion of the entire tokenization lifecycle within one unified platform - from asset structuring and issuance to investment and management.

This update enables businesses to:

  • Issue tokenized assets directly on the blockchain through Stobox 4.
  • Onboard investors through a fully compliant KYC/AML process.
  • Manage investments and track performance in real time through the integrated Stobox Wallet.

For investors, this means a new level of transparency and accessibility. Tokenized assets can now be viewed, held, and managed directly in the Stobox 4 Wallet, providing a direct and secure way to participate in digital ownership.

First Tokenized Assets Are Live

We’re proud to share that the first tokenized assets of Stobox clients are now available via the Stobox 4 Wallet.

These projects represent diverse industries – from real estate and private equity to commodities and fintech. Each offering is structured with compliance and investor protection at its core, using Stobox’s proprietary infrastructure:

  • Stobox DID for digital identity verification
  • STV3 Smart Contracts for secure, upgradable blockchain issuance

With these components working together, Stobox 4 is now a complete environment for compliant digital investment.

Stobox Leads by Example: Tokenizing Its Own Shares

To demonstrate the power of the technology we’ve built, Stobox has tokenized its own equity. The company is issuing its shares as STBX Security Tokens on the Arbitrum blockchain.

This marks a historic step for Stobox - and for the broader tokenization industry, as we apply the same principles and infrastructure we deliver to our clients to our own business.

STBX represents an equity-linked participation in Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd., providing investors with an opportunity to own a share in the company building the future of tokenized finance.

The tokens are issued and managed via the Stobox 4 Platform, fully integrated with compliance tools and investor verification through Sumsub.

Investors can join the STBX Whitelist now through assets.stobox.io, complete KYC, and prepare to participate in the upcoming offering.

Why This Matters

The launch of live tokenized assets on Stobox 4 is not just another product release - it’s the realization of the vision Stobox has pursued since its inception:

With this update, tokenization moves from concept to practice. Businesses can raise capital through digital assets, investors can access new opportunities, and both can trust the compliance and technology behind it.

Stobox Security Token (STBX) Overview

  • Issuer: Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd (BVI)
  • Parent Company: Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd.
  • KYB Verification: Completed by Sumsub, a global KYC/KYB compliance provider.
  • Verification & Asset Data: Publicly available on the STBX Asset Page
  • Access: Investor offering documents and data are available only after registration on Stobox 4 and through access via the Stobox 4 Wallet
  • Network: Arbitrum One
  • Token Standard: ERC-20 (STV3 Protocol Architecture)
  • Accepted Currency: USDC (on Arbitrum) - the only stablecoin accepted for the STO at this time
  • Future Upgrade: By the end of Q4, Stobox plans to enable conversion of STBU utility tokens into STBX, giving existing token holders a regulated participation option.

️ 2,000,000 STBX tokens have been issued, representing 20% of the equity participation in Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd. Each token is backed by a participation certificate tied to Class-C shares of the company.

Alternative to Venture Capital

STBX is an alternative to traditional venture funding, opening access to both accredited and public investors under regulatory exemptions, including:

  • Regulation D (U.S.) for accredited investors
  • Small-sized offering exemptions in select jurisdictions

This model aligns with Stobox’s vision of democratizing private equity, enabling transparent, cross-border participation in a company building the infrastructure of tokenized capital markets.

What’s Next

By the end of Q4, Stobox plans to introduce conversion of STBU Utility Tokens to STBX, enabling existing token holders to transition into regulated, equity-linked participation.

We’ll also continue expanding partnerships, bringing more issuers, investors, and service providers into the Stobox ecosystem.

Our roadmap remains focused on building the global infrastructure for tokenized finance – one update, one client, and one milestone at a time.

Join the Future of Tokenization

Stobox invites investors, businesses, and partners to experience the next chapter in digital finance:

  • Explore live assets on Stobox 4
  • Join the STBX Whitelist to become part of Stobox’s growth
  • Follow our updates as we continue to lead the tokenization revolution
