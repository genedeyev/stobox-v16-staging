What Stobox is

Who we are – and what we are not

Stobox is a technology provider that builds software and infrastructure for tokenizing real-world assets. We are not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, custodian, exchange, or law firm, and we do not provide investment, legal, financial, or tax advice.

  • Non-custodial. We never take custody of your assets, funds, or private keys, and we are never in the flow of funds.
  • Regulated activity runs through licensed partners. Securities offerings are conducted by licensed broker-dealers; offering documents are finalized by issuers' counsel.
  • No percentage of any offering. Stobox charges flat technology fees; any success fee on a raise is charged by the licensed broker-dealer.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to provide the services – account details, usage data, and, where required for compliance, identity verification handled by specialized providers. For verification we rely on attestations: we read a verified yes/no, and do not store your raw identity documents.

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Identity verification is performed by third-party providers; on-chain attestations record the result, not your documents.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

Compliance

AML, KYC & sanctions

Access to regulated products and offerings requires identity verification. Verification and screening are performed by specialized providers; for regulated offerings, eligibility is determined by the licensed broker-dealer.

  • Identity (KYC) and, where relevant, business (KYB) verification is required before participating in regulated offerings.
  • We rely on on-chain attestations: a verified result is recorded, not your raw identity documents.
  • We screen against applicable sanctions and prohibited-jurisdiction lists; we do not serve restricted persons or territories.
  • Suspicious activity may be reported to the relevant authorities as required by law.

Who you're dealing with

Corporate structure

Stobox operates through a group of companies, each with a defined role. The Stobox technology platform is provided by the Stobox group; regulated securities are issued and distributed through the appropriate licensed entities and partners.

  • Stobox Technologies – develops and provides the tokenization software and platform.
  • Raisable – the capital-raising product, working alongside licensed broker-dealers.
  • Stobox Tokenized Equities Ltd – the issuer associated with STBX, the Stobox security token.
  • Stobox Innovations Ltd – the issuer associated with STBU, the Stobox utility token.
  • Regulated sales are conducted by licensed broker-dealers (e.g., tZERO, Texture Capital, Silicon Prairie), not by Stobox.

Entity names and roles are indicative; the official Offering Documents and agreements govern.
